# Business Insights & Recommendations

## 1. Objective

The objective of this analysis is to translate supply chain performance metrics into actionable business insights.

The analysis focuses on identifying delivery inefficiencies, customer-level performance gaps and opportunities to improve order fulfilment.

## 2. Key Areas of Investigation

### Customer Performance

- Identify customers with the lowest OTIF performance.
- Compare customer delivery performance against assigned targets.
- Identify customers experiencing recurring fulfilment issues.

### Product Performance

- Analyze product-level order quantities and delivered quantities.
- Identify products with lower fulfilment rates.
- Investigate potential patterns in incomplete deliveries.

### Time-Based Analysis

- Monitor monthly OTIF, OT and IF performance.
- Identify periods of declining delivery performance.
- Compare performance across different time periods.

### Target Performance

- Compare actual delivery KPIs against customer-specific targets.
- Identify customers with the largest performance gaps.
- Prioritize areas requiring operational improvements.

## 3. Business Recommendations Framework

| Finding to Investigate | Potential Business Action |
|---|---|
| Low customer OTIF | Review delivery processes for affected customers |
| High incomplete order frequency | Investigate product availability and fulfilment processes |
| Declining monthly OT performance | Review delivery scheduling and dispatch efficiency |
| Large target-performance gap | Prioritize improvement plans for underperforming customers |
| Low volume fill rate | Investigate quantity fulfilment issues |

## 4. Analytical Approach

The recommendations will be developed using:

- Customer-level KPI comparisons.
- Product-level fulfilment analysis.
- Monthly delivery trends.
- Actual versus target performance.
- Order-level and order-line-level metrics.
