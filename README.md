# Supply Chain & Logistics Analytics

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing supply chain operations for AtliQ Mart, a fictional FMCG company. The objective is to evaluate delivery performance, identify operational inefficiencies, and understand customer-level order fulfillment.

The analysis is based on the AtliQ Mart Supply Chain Analytics challenge by Codebasics.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze On-Time Delivery (OT), In-Full Delivery (IF), and OTIF performance.
- Evaluate delivery performance against customer-specific targets.
- Identify customers experiencing frequent delivery issues.
- Analyze product-level fulfillment performance.
- Track monthly trends in supply chain KPIs.
- Generate actionable recommendations to improve operational efficiency.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

**1. On-Time Delivery (OT%)**

Measures the percentage of orders delivered on or before the promised delivery date.

**2. In-Full Delivery (IF%)**

Measures the percentage of orders delivered with the complete requested quantity.

**3. On-Time In-Full (OTIF%)**

Measures the percentage of orders that satisfy both on-time and in-full delivery conditions.

**4. Line Fill Rate (LIFR%)**

Measures the proportion of order lines fulfilled completely.

**5. Volume Fill Rate (VOFR%)**

Measures the proportion of ordered quantity successfully fulfilled.
